01Open the desk

Run syneva next to your repository. The diff opens on localhost, in your browser — nothing leaves your machine.

02Read with a guide

Your agent attaches a reading order and groups the files into sections. Every file stays visible, listed or not.

03Ask on the line

Questions anchor to the exact change. Your agent answers in the same thread, without you leaving the desk.

04Send the verdict

Accept or reject each change. Your agent receives a structured result and re-diffs its edits into the same tab.

Anything your agent rewrites resets to pending. Anything you settled stays settled.

The verdict,
as a contract.

Send to Agent hands back a structured ReviewResult — printed to stdout and written to disk, readable by any harness. Not a screenshot, not a vibe: your agent knows exactly what you accepted, what you rejected, and what you asked.

It acts on the round, calls syneva reload, and the same tab shows its next revision — your settled decisions carried over, its edits back to pending.

A few things
worth knowing.

Does Syneva review code for me?

No. You review the code. Your own agent can organize the reading order and answer questions. Syneva records your decisions and hands your feedback back to that agent.

Does Syneva ever auto-approve anything?

No. Every change starts pending, and only your click records a decision. A decision is a verdict stored on that exact change — not git staging, not a blanket approval of a file.

What if the agent rewrites code I already accepted?

Syneva hashes the content of each change. On reload, a rewritten change resets to pending; untouched work keeps your verdict. Approvals and comment anchors follow the same rule, so stale trust can't survive silently.

Which coding agents can I use?

The CLI uses a documented JSON and localhost HTTP contract. It includes a pi integration, and other agents can use the standalone CLI. See the setup guide for the commands your agent needs.

Can I use the plan desk today?

The dedicated plan desk is a prototype. The current CLI can open a file for review, including a plan, but the claim map and risk-ranked queue shown here are not shipped yet.

Where does my review live?

On your machine, under ~/.syneva. The desk binds to localhost by default and saves decisions between rounds. There is no hosted account or telemetry.
